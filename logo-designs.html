1. Tilt and glare

The logo turns toward your cursor in 3D with a moving highlight sliding over the gold.

2. Gold shimmer

A band of light sweeps across the letters on its own, and faster when you hover.

3. Embers

Sparks rise off the letters wherever your cursor passes.

4. Petals

The logo is made of tiny petals. Move through it and they scatter and spin, then settle back.

5. Spotlight

The logo waits in the dark; your cursor (or finger) is the spotlight.

6. 3D extrude

The logo is a thick solid block that turns as you move.

7. Glitch

Hover (or tap) and the logo splits into colour layers and jitters like a broken screen.

8. Light the diya

Click the diya. The flame catches, light floods out and the logo glows to life.

9. Liquid gold

The gold ripples like molten metal wherever you move the cursor.

10. Sparkle trail

Glitter follows your cursor across the letters. Click for a burst of petals.
