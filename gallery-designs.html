1. Expanding strips

Hover or tap a strip and it opens up while the others fold away. Click the open one to view it full size.

2. Photo deck

A pile of prints. Click the top photo (or swipe it) to flick it to the back.

3. Coverflow

Photos fan out in 3D. Click a side photo, use the arrows or keys, or swipe.

4. Cinema reel

One big photo with a slow zoom, auto-advancing. Pick any thumbnail; hovering pauses it.

5. Filter mosaic

A mixed-size grid. Filter by theme, hover for the label, click to view.

6. Rangoli ring

Photos orbit a turning rangoli. Click one and it rolls to the top and fills the centre.

7. Hanging line

Prints pegged on a line and swinging gently. Hover to catch one; click to view.
