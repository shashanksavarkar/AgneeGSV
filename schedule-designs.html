1. Glowing timeline

A centre line that lights up as you scroll. Acts slide in from alternate sides and each diya ignites.

2. Time scrubber

Drag the clock (or press play). Finished acts fade, the current one glows, the next one is flagged.

3. Admit-one tickets

Each act is a perforated ticket that tilts toward your cursor.

4. Card rail

Swipe, drag, scroll or use the arrows. The card in the middle grows.

5. Evening dial

The whole evening as a ring, each arc sized by its length. Hover or tap an arc to read it.

6. Stage Gantt

Bars on a time axis, one row per block, so you can see how long each act runs. Hover or tap a bar.

7. Block accordion

Six blocks, one open at a time, each with its time span and a fill bar showing how much of the night it takes.

8. Flip cards

The time on the front; hover or tap to turn it over for the act and its length.

9. Show player

A music-player style running order. Press play and it steps through the acts (one minute plays as half a second).

10. Path of fire

A winding path. A flame travels down it as you scroll and lights each diya.
