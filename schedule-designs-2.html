11. Split-flap board

An old station departures board. The letters flip into place as you reach it. Press Shuffle, or hover a row, to flip again.

12. Vinyl record

A spinning record. Pick an act and the needle swings across to its groove while the label changes.

13. Stage spotlight

The running order sits in the dark. Move your cursor (or drag a finger) and the spotlight reveals it.

14. Stacking cards

Scroll down: every act drops onto the pile like a card dealt onto a table.

15. Giant type

Huge outlined names that fill with fire as you hover, with the slot length following your cursor.

16. Orbit

Each block is an orbit around the flame and its acts circle on it. Hover to freeze and read.

17. Constellation

The evening as a chain of stars. Hover a star to read it; click one to light the line up to it.

18. 3D drum

All the acts on a rotating drum. Drag to spin it; it turns on its own when you let go.

19. Night sky

Slide time forward and the sky goes from dusk to midnight while the current act shows in the clouds.

20. Embers

Run a cursor over the list and the lines catch fire, throwing sparks.

21. Ticker tape

Two bands of acts sliding past in opposite directions, like a stock ticker. Hover to freeze, click a chip to read it.

22. Desk calendar

A tear-off desk calendar. Click the page (or the buttons) and it flips up on its rings to show the next act.

23. Petal picker

Six petals of a rangoli, one per block. Pick a petal and its acts bloom beside it.

24. Scratch cards

Every act is hidden under gold foil. Scratch it with your mouse or finger to reveal what is on.

25. Radar

A sweeping radar. Each act is a blip placed by its time; the beam makes them ping as it passes. Hover or tap a blip.
